Armen Enikolopov, PhD

aenikolopov@gmail.com

I’m a neuroscientist and field botanist based in New York. I did my PhD at Columbia. I’ve worked on smell, brain–computer interfaces and immunopsychiatry, led neurobiology discovery at Imprint and co-founded Canaery, now General Sense.

In the Andes and the Chocó rainforest I study tropical plants and model climate and biomes. I’ve led eleven expeditions and described two new species; another is named after me. Photos are on Instagram.
